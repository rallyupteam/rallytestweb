the challenge

a founder starting a new company has the hardest content job there is: build an audience for a product nobody can see yet, then make launch day count.

he already had a following, but he posted about once every few weeks. he hired rallyup with one goal, in his words: "scale our linkedin content engine."

the solution

one dedicated rallyup writer worked with him from july to november 2026. we built the context first: his story, how he thinks about ai for small businesses, and what letsgo would launch. then the writer turned it into posts, every one approved in slack before it went live.

✓

the story before the product. during stealth, posts were about him: where he came from, what he learned leading teams, and why he left a role he loved to start again.

✓

a cadence the algorithm notices. posting frequency went up 3.7x in the first 10 weeks, so each post had an audience already warmed up by the last one.

✓

a launch with a story around it. not one announcement but several posts: why he left a role he loved, what letsgo is for, and the waitlist opening. the first 100 spots filled in under 24 hours, and hundreds signed up.

✓

a pivot, told in public. when letsgo moved from consumers to businesses, we helped him tell the pivot as it happened: what they learned, who letsgo is for now, and a free ai audit for teams. business leaders started reaching out within 48 hours, and letsgo signed its first enterprise customers after the pivot.

the results

reach per post more than doubled in the first 10 weeks, so when he announced letsgo the audience was already there: the launch post drew 849 engagements, and hundreds signed up for the waitlist. after the pivot to businesses, that same audience brought in letsgo's first enterprise customers.
