what's different about linkedin for ai startups

  • The irony test. Buyers of AI products spot AI-written posts instantly. Posts need a human writer and real opinions, or they undercut the product.
  • Speed matters. The AI market moves weekly. Your executives need to react to launches and news while it is still relevant.
  • Technical credibility. Developers, CTOs and investors want specifics: what you shipped, what broke, what you learned.
  • Hiring pull. A visible founder is one of the strongest recruiting channels a young AI company has.

frequently asked questions

should an ai company use ai to write its founder's linkedin posts?

Use AI for research, not for the writing. Readers in AI are the quickest to spot generated text. rallyup uses AI only for research; every post is written by a human in the founder's voice.

what should ai founders post on linkedin?

Launches and what went into them, honest takes on where the market is going, customer stories, and lessons from building. Opinions backed by specifics outperform announcements.

does linkedin content help ai startups raise or hire?

Often, yes. Investors and candidates look founders up before a meeting, and an active profile with clear opinions gives them a reason to reach out.
