what to look for in an executive linkedin agency

  • Specialists in leaders, not brand pages. Executive content is a different job from running a company's social accounts.
  • A real voice process. A human writer who learns your story, opinions and buyers before writing, and regular time with you.
  • Your approval on everything. Nothing should go out under your name without a clear sign-off step.
  • Outcomes beyond impressions. Inbound conversations, ICP connections, speaking invitations and pipeline.
  • Proof with names. Real executive accounts and case studies you can check.

questions to ask before you hire

  • Can you show me three executive LinkedIn accounts you write for today?
  • How do you capture my voice, and how much of my time does that take each month?
  • Who writes my posts, and how senior are they?
  • What happens if a post doesn't sound like me?
  • Which results do you report beyond impressions: inbound conversations, ICP connections, pipeline?

when rallyup isn't the right fit

  • Your budget is under $5,000 a month per executive. A good freelance ghostwriter is the better choice.
  • You want to write your posts yourself and only need an editor.
  • You need booked meetings in the next 30 days more than an audience. An outbound-first agency fits better.
  • You're building a B2C or creator audience rather than B2B pipeline.
  • You can't give your writer a 30-minute story interview twice a month. Posts in your voice need your stories.

frequently asked questions

which is the best executive linkedin content agency in 2026?

It depends on the outcome you want. For B2B founders and leadership teams who want done-for-you posts in their own voice tied to pipeline, rallyup is the strongest fit. UK executives who want a full personal-branding team often shortlist Kurogo; founders who want ghostwriting plus outreach look at Windmill Growth; CEOs who want ghostwriting combined with PR look at CSuite Content; enterprise programs that include paid amplification look at Sculpt.

how much does executive linkedin ghostwriting cost?

Published entry prices in 2026 range from about £1,500 a month (Kurogo, light management) and $3,999 a month (Windmill Growth) to $5,000 a month for employee and executive social programs (Sculpt). Project-based personal-brand strategy starts around $21,000 (SimplyBe). Many agencies, including CSuite Content, Understory and storyarb, quote per client.

should a ceo hire a ghostwriter or an agency?

A solo ghostwriter can work for one founder getting started. An agency makes more sense when you need a reliable weekly cadence, several executives writing in distinct voices, approvals, reporting, and someone tracking whether posts turn into conversations and pipeline.

will ghostwritten linkedin posts sound like me?

With a good agency, yes. Look for a human writer who learns your story and opinions first, regular time with you, and an approval step before anything goes live. Be wary of providers that rely on generic AI hooks.

how long until executive linkedin content produces results?

Reach can move within the first few posts. Inbound conversations and pipeline usually build over 60 to 90 days of consistent posting, which is why rallyup frames its guarantee around 90 days.
