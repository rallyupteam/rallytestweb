what audience building actually involves

Posting is only one part of it. An executive audience that turns into pipeline is built from four pieces, and most services cover some of them, not all.

  • Content that earns attention. Posts drawn from the executive's own stories, opinions and market, published on a steady cadence. Consistency matters more than any single post.
  • A profile that converts. Every good post sends people to the profile. If the headline and About section don't say who you help and what to do next, that attention leaks away.
  • The right audience, not just a bigger one. Followers from your industry, your buyers and your partners are worth far more than a large general following. Targeted connection requests to your ideal customer profile (ICP) speed that up.
  • Measuring who the audience is. Follower counts and impressions don't tell you whether buyers are paying attention. Look for reporting on ICP connections, profile views and inbound messages.

If an executive is getting reach but nothing comes of it, one of the last three pieces is usually missing. Our diagnostic on linkedin posts getting views but no leads walks through how to find which one.

what growth realistically looks like

In our executive linkedin benchmarks 2026 report, which covers 14 B2B executives on rallyup's program from May to November 2026:

  • Followers grew a median 17.4% in about 90 days (9 executives with comparable data).
  • Monthly impressions grew a median 4.7x in the first 90 days compared with the 90 days before (7 executives with complete account data), and 6 of those 7 at least doubled.
  • Consistency drove most of it. Median cadence went from 22 to 31 posts per 90 days, and the top 10% of posts produced 75% of impressions. Posts above 50,000 impressions went from 0% to 4.5%.

how to vet a service

  • Ask how they grow the audience beyond posting. Do they rewrite the profile? Do they send connection requests to your ICP, or only hope the right people find you?
  • Ask what the report shows. Request a sample. Impressions and followers are a start; look for ICP connections, profile views and inbound messages.
  • Ask who writes, and how they get your material. A dedicated writer and regular interviews keep posts in your voice. A single questionnaire runs dry.
  • Ask how approvals work. Nothing should go out under your name without your sign-off.
  • Ask for results from someone like you. Named companies and real numbers, in your industry or at your stage.
  • Ask what happens if the numbers don't move. rallyup guarantees 100k organic impressions in 90 days, or we keep working for free until you get there, for B2B founders and executives with $5m+ in ARR or funding. Ask any other service whether it offers anything similar.

when rallyup isn't the right fit

  • Your budget is under $5,000 a month per executive. A good freelance ghostwriter is the better choice.
  • You want to write your posts yourself and only need an editor or a coach.
  • You need booked meetings in the next 30 days more than an audience. An outbound-first agency fits better.
  • You're building a B2C or creator audience rather than B2B pipeline.
  • You can't give your writer a 30-minute story interview twice a month. Posts in your voice need your stories.

frequently asked questions

what are the best services for executive linkedin audience building?

It depends on what you need. For B2B founders and leadership teams who want posts, a profile rewrite, ICP connection requests and reporting done for them, rallyup is the strongest fit. Windmill Growth suits a single founder who wants outreach and inbox management bundled in, Kurogo suits UK executives who want carousels, graphics and PR, and Sculpt suits enterprise programs that include paid amplification.

how fast can an executive grow a linkedin audience?

Across the executives in rallyup's executive LinkedIn benchmarks 2026, followers grew a median 17.4% in about 90 days (9 executives) and monthly impressions grew a median 4.7x in the first 90 days (7 executives). Reach can move within the first few posts; audience and inbound build over a quarter of consistent posting.

is follower count the right way to measure audience building?

Only partly. A smaller audience of buyers, partners and peers in your industry is worth more than a large general following. Look for reporting on ICP connections, profile views and inbound messages alongside followers and impressions.

do audience building services send connection requests for you?

Many do. rallyup includes automated ICP connection requests in every program, Windmill Growth's Founder OS includes ICP outreach, CSuite Content's entry tier includes connection outreach and storyarb includes targeted outreach. Ask each service how it picks who to connect with.

how much does executive linkedin audience building cost?

Published starting prices checked in November 2026 include Kurogo from £1,500 a month, Windmill Growth at $3,999 a month for Founder OS, and Sculpt and rallyup from $5,000 a month per executive. CSuite Content, storyarb and Understory quote per client.

how much of an executive's time does it take?

With rallyup it is about 25 minutes for the onboarding form, a 30-minute session on the first drafts, a story interview twice a month and a few minutes a week approving posts in Slack.
