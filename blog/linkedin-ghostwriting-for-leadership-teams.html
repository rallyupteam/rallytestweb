how team ghostwriting works

  • One writer per executive. Voice depends on continuity. With rallyup, each executive has a dedicated human writer and a story interview twice a month.
  • Separate approvals. Each executive approves, edits or rejects their own posts in Slack. Nobody publishes in someone else's name without sign-off.
  • A shared calendar. When the company launches something, the team's posts can cover it from different angles on different days, instead of four executives sharing the same announcement.
  • One report. Impressions, follower growth and ICP connection requests per executive, so you can see who is driving what.

give each executive a lane

RoleNatural laneWho it reaches
Founder or CEOMarket point of view, company story, hard decisionsBuyers, investors, candidates

Product or technical leaderThe problem you solve, how it is built, what's nextTechnical buyers and candidates

These lanes are a starting point, not a rule. The lane should come from what each executive actually knows and talks about in their interviews.

start with one, then roll out

Most rallyup clients start with one executive, see it work, then roll it out to the rest of the leadership team. The first executive builds the internal proof, and the next ones start with a clear picture of how little time it takes: about 25 minutes for the onboarding form, a 30-minute session on the first drafts, a story interview twice a month and a few minutes a week approving posts.

when rallyup isn't the right fit

  • Your budget is under $5,000 a month per executive.
  • You want many employees resharing company content. An employee advocacy program fits better.
  • You mainly want company-page content rather than posts from named executives.
  • Your goals are B2C or creator growth. We write for B2B founders and executives selling to other businesses.
  • Your executives can't give a 30-minute story interview twice a month. That conversation is where the posts come from.

frequently asked questions

can an agency write linkedin posts for my whole leadership team?

Yes. The approach that works is one dedicated writer per executive, so each voice stays distinct, plus a shared plan so the team's posts support each other instead of repeating each other. rallyup prices per executive, from $5,000 per executive per month.

should we start with one executive or the whole team?

Most companies start with one executive, usually the founder or CEO, see it work, then roll it out to the rest of the leadership team. Starting with one keeps the first quarter simple and gives the rest of the team proof before they commit time.

how do you keep several executives from sounding the same?

Give each executive their own writer and their own story interviews, and give each a distinct lane: the CEO on market and vision, a sales leader on buyers, a product leader on the problem. Voices blur when one writer drafts for everyone from the same brief.

how much does linkedin ghostwriting for a leadership team cost?

Most agencies price per executive. rallyup starts at $5,000 per executive per month. Published entry prices across agencies run from about £1,500 to $5,000 a month per executive; see our cost guide for the list.

is executive ghostwriting the same as an employee advocacy program?

No. Employee advocacy usually means many employees resharing company content. Executive ghostwriting means a few leaders publishing original posts in their own voice. Some agencies run both; rallyup focuses on founders and executives.

what results can a leadership team expect?

At PandaDoc, rallyup's work produced 1,235 engagements across 3 executives in one month, and one executive's first post reached 27,000+ impressions. In our 2026 benchmark (14 executives), the 7 with complete account data grew monthly impressions a median 4.7x in the first 90 days.
