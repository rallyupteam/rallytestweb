the short answer

Windmill Growth is a strong choice for a single B2B founder who wants content, outbound and inbox management from one team for $3,999 a month. If you are comparing alternatives, the right one depends on what you need. rallyup is the strongest fit for B2B founders and leadership teams who want several executives posting in their own voices, with a dedicated human writer each and a written 100k-impression guarantee. Kurogo suits UK executives who want carousels, graphics and PR. Outbound-first agencies such as Media Engine and SalesBread suit teams that care most about booked meetings.

what windmill growth offers

Windmill Growth is a New York LinkedIn ghostwriting and founder-branding agency for B2B founders. It says it has worked with more than 185 founders and keeps about 30 on the books at a time. Before onboarding, it reviews a founder's existing podcasts, press and posts, defines the ideal customer profile, and matches the founder with a writer from a bench of industry specialists. Outbound runs through traxy.ai, which finds ICP-matching profiles engaging with your content or your competitors' and adds them to outreach sequences.

who windmill fits best

Windmill is built for one founder at a time who wants the whole LinkedIn channel run for them: posts, outreach and replies, with meetings landing in the calendar. If that is you, and your industry is one Windmill already writes for, it is worth a call. Its weekly interview rhythm and up to five posts a week suit founders who want high volume.

why buyers compare alternatives

  • More than one executive. Founder OS is priced and designed around a founder. If your CEO, CRO and CMO all need to post, look for per-executive programs built for teams.
  • Industry fit. Windmill says it only accepts founders in industries where it already has expertise, so some companies will be outside its bench.
  • Timing. Windmill takes three new clients a month, so a start date can depend on capacity.
  • Content without outbound. Some teams already run outbound in-house and want writing, publishing and audience building only.
  • Region and format. UK teams, or teams that want carousels and graphics as standard, may prefer an agency built around that.

the alternatives

1. rallyup: best for founders and leadership teams

rallyup is a done-for-you LinkedIn ghostwriting agency for B2B founders and executives. Each executive gets a dedicated human writer, three posts a week published at peak times, a story interview twice a month and a profile rewrite, plus automated ICP connection requests and a monthly progress report. Every post is approved in Slack before it goes live. AI is used for research, never for the writing. Pricing starts at $5,000 per executive per month, with a guarantee of 100k organic impressions in 90 days or you don't pay for B2B founders and executives with $5m+ in ARR or funding.

Across 14 B2B executives in our executive linkedin benchmarks 2026, the median executive's monthly impressions grew 4.7x in the first 90 days compared with the 90 days before, and 6 of the 7 executives with complete data at least doubled. Followers grew a median 17.4% in about 90 days. Named results are on our case studies page, including an Airwallex program that reached 701,198 impressions across 39 posts in two and a half months.

2. Kurogo: best for UK executives who want design and PR

Kurogo is a London personal branding agency. Its ghostwriting plans include 10 to 20 posts a month with carousels and graphics as standard, from £1,500 a month for light management or £2,500 for full management, and it also offers PR and lead generation. See our kurogo alternatives page for the full breakdown.

3. Media Engine: best for booked meetings

A pipeline-focused agency that bundles ghostwriting with outreach, commenting and appointment setting. A fit if meetings matter more to you than audience growth.

4. SalesBread: best for outbound-led teams

Started as a B2B LinkedIn outreach agency and added ghostwriting. A fit when outbound is the main motion and content supports it.

5. CSuite Content: best for CEOs who want PR too

Combines LinkedIn ghostwriting (about 8 to 10 posts a month) with op-eds, speeches and earned media. Pricing is quoted per client.

6. A freelance ghostwriter: best for smaller budgets

If you are one executive with time to brief and manage a writer yourself, a freelancer can cost less. Our guide to ghostwriter vs agency covers the trade-offs.

how to choose

  • How many people will post? One founder points to Windmill or a freelancer. Several executives point to a per-executive team program such as rallyup.
  • Do you already run outbound? If yes, pay for content and audience building, not a second outbound engine.
  • How much time can you give? Compare a weekly interview with a twice-monthly one, and ask how approvals work.
  • What will you measure? Ask each agency for a sample report and for results from someone in your industry.

when rallyup isn't the right fit

  • Your budget is under $5,000 a month per executive.
  • You want to write it yourself and just need editing.
  • You need meetings in the next 30 days rather than an audience that produces them over a quarter.
  • Your goals are B2C or creator growth. We write for B2B founders and executives selling to other businesses.
  • You can't give a 30-minute story interview twice a month. That conversation is where the posts come from.

B2B founders who want one team to handle their LinkedIn content, outbound and inbox together. Windmill matches each founder with a writer who specializes in their industry and says it only accepts founders in industries where it already has expertise.
