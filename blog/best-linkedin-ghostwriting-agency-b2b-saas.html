what's different about linkedin for b2b saas

  • Pipeline, not likes. Track ICP connection requests, profile views and inbound DMs, not just impressions.
  • The whole leadership team. CEO, CRO and CPO each reach a different buyer. Programs priced per executive make it easy to start with one.
  • Buyers research before they talk to sales. An active exec profile is part of your buying journey whether you manage it or not.
  • Consistency beats virality. Three good posts a week, every week, compounds; one viral post doesn't.

when rallyup isn't the right fit

  • Your budget is under $5,000 a month per executive. A good freelance ghostwriter is the better choice.
  • You want to write your posts yourself and only need an editor.
  • You need booked meetings in the next 30 days more than an audience. An outbound-first agency fits better.
  • You're building a B2C or creator audience rather than B2B pipeline.
  • You can't give your writer a 30-minute story interview twice a month. Posts in your voice need your stories.
