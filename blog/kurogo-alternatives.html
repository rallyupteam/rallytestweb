what kurogo offers

Kurogo is a London personal branding agency. It says its team has written more than 60,000 posts for over 400 founders, executives and experts. Its services include LinkedIn strategy, ghostwriting, founder branding, executive thought leadership, PR and LinkedIn lead generation.

Ghostwriting starts with a long onboarding interview, then a positioning strategy and voice guide, an idea bank and a line-by-line review of the first two weeks of drafts. After that, the client's time is a 60-minute idea call a month and about 30 minutes a week approving drafts in a shared calendar. Posts are written by in-house human copywriters, with AI used for research.

who kurogo fits best

Kurogo fits founders and executives who want a design-rich LinkedIn presence, with carousels and graphics in every plan, and who value a UK-based team with PR and speaking opportunities on offer. Its done-with-you programme is also a good option for executives who would rather write their own posts with coaching.

why buyers compare alternatives

  • A whole leadership team. If several executives will post, compare per-executive programs built around a dedicated writer for each person.
  • Interview rhythm. Some executives prefer shorter, more frequent conversations to a monthly idea call.
  • Where approvals happen. Kurogo uses a shared calendar. Teams that live in Slack may prefer approving there.
  • A written guarantee. Some buyers want a published outcome guarantee before committing.
  • Outbound bundled in. Founders who want outreach and inbox management in the same plan may look at Windmill Growth.

the alternatives

1. rallyup: best for founders and leadership teams

rallyup is a done-for-you LinkedIn ghostwriting agency for B2B founders and executives. Each executive gets a dedicated human writer, three posts a week published at peak times, a 30-minute story interview twice a month and a profile rewrite, plus automated ICP connection requests and a monthly progress report. Every post is approved in Slack before it goes live. AI is used for research, never for the writing. Pricing starts at $5,000 per executive per month, with a guarantee of 100k organic impressions in 90 days or you don't pay for B2B founders and executives with $5m+ in ARR or funding.

Across 14 B2B executives in our executive linkedin benchmarks 2026, the median executive's monthly impressions grew 4.7x in the first 90 days compared with the 90 days before, and 6 of the 7 executives with complete data at least doubled. Followers grew a median 17.4% in about 90 days. Named results are on our case studies page, including an Airwallex program that reached 701,198 impressions across 39 posts in two and a half months.

2. Windmill Growth: best for a single founder who wants outbound too

A New York agency for B2B founders. Its Founder OS plan ($3,999 a month) includes a weekly 30-minute interview, up to five posts a week, a profile rebuild, ICP outreach and inbox management. See our windmill growth alternatives page for the full breakdown.

3. CSuite Content: best for CEOs who want PR too

Combines LinkedIn ghostwriting (about 8 to 10 posts a month) with op-eds, speeches and earned media. Pricing is quoted per client.

4. Sculpt: best for enterprise programs

Runs employee and executive social programs including executive ghostwriting, video, workshops and Thought Leader Ads, from $5,000 a month.

5. Media Engine: best for booked meetings

A pipeline-focused agency that bundles ghostwriting with outreach, commenting and appointment setting.

6. A freelance ghostwriter: best for smaller budgets

If you are one executive with time to brief and manage a writer yourself, a freelancer can cost less. Our guide to ghostwriter vs agency covers the trade-offs.

how to choose

  • How many people will post? For several executives, compare per-executive pricing and whether each person gets their own writer.
  • How do you want to give input? A monthly idea call, a twice-monthly interview or a weekly one: pick the rhythm you will actually keep.
  • Do you need design and PR? If carousels and press matter most, weight those. If audience and pipeline matter most, weight cadence and reporting.
  • What will you measure? Ask each agency for a sample report and for results from someone in your industry.

when rallyup isn't the right fit

  • Your budget is under $5,000 a month per executive.
  • You want to write it yourself and just need editing.
  • You need meetings in the next 30 days rather than an audience that produces them over a quarter.
  • Your goals are B2C or creator growth. We write for B2B founders and executives selling to other businesses.
  • You can't give a 30-minute story interview twice a month. That conversation is where the posts come from.

frequently asked questions

what are the best kurogo alternatives?

It depends on what you need. For B2B founders and leadership teams who want several executives posting in their own voices with a dedicated writer each, rallyup is the strongest fit. Single B2B founders who want content, outreach and inbox management together look at Windmill Growth. CEOs who want ghostwriting combined with PR look at CSuite Content, and outbound-led teams look at Media Engine or SalesBread.

how much does kurogo cost?

As published on kurogo.co.uk in November 2026: done-for-you ghostwriting starts from £1,500 a month for light management or £2,500 a month for full management, with 10 to 20 posts a month depending on scope. A six-month done-with-you consulting programme costs £2,500.

who is kurogo best for?

Founders, CEOs and experts, especially in the UK, who want a full personal-branding team: positioning, ghostwriting with carousels and graphics as standard, and optional PR and lead generation. Kurogo says its team has written more than 60,000 posts for over 400 founders, executives and experts.

how is rallyup different from kurogo?

rallyup is built around a dedicated human writer per executive, three posts a week, a 30-minute story interview twice a month and approvals in Slack, with ICP connection requests and a profile rewrite included. Pricing starts at $5,000 per executive per month, and qualifying B2B executives get a guarantee of 100k organic impressions in 90 days or they don't pay.

does kurogo use ai to write posts?

Kurogo says its writers use AI for research and organizing ideas, and every post is written and edited by an in-house human copywriter. rallyup works the same way: AI for research, never for the writing.
