what understory offers

Understory describes itself as an "allbound B2B growth agency for SaaS": paid media, GTM engineering (signal-based outbound built in Clay), LinkedIn content and RevOps, run as one system. It lists No. 140 on the 2026 Inc. 5000 and says it is trusted by 150+ B2B companies.

Its LinkedIn content service is for founders and executive teams at growth-stage and enterprise B2B SaaS companies. As its page describes the process:

  • An intake form, a Slack channel and a Notion board at kickoff, plus a profile audit.
  • Research that scores 16 content styles by what lands with each buyer persona.
  • A voice interview every two weeks, led by your writer.
  • 16 or 30 posts a month, drafted about two weeks out in Ordinal, with text, carousels, infographics and your photos, and up to two rounds of revisions per post.
  • Winning posts run as thought-leader ads; ICP-fit engagers go into outreach as an add-on.

Its featured content result: Operatus, where a 90-minute interview each month with the CEO becomes 10 to 20 posts, most posts reach 5,000 to 10,000 impressions, the best reached 50,000, and engagement grew 600% in 90 days. Understory does not publish its prices; a call sets the scope.

who understory fits best

Understory fits a SaaS company that wants one partner for the whole demand engine: LinkedIn content plus paid ads, outbound and CRM work, with content amplified as ads. If you plan to buy several of those services anyway, keeping them under one team is a real advantage, and its content volume (16 or 30 posts a month) suits executives who want a high cadence.

why buyers compare alternatives

  • Content only. Teams that already run paid and outbound in-house often want executive content and audience building without a wider growth retainer.
  • A published price. Understory quotes per client. Some buyers want to compare a fixed, published price before a call.
  • Not SaaS. Understory's LinkedIn page is written for B2B SaaS. Fintech, VC and other B2B executives may want an agency with results in their space.
  • Volume. 16 to 30 posts a month is a lot of approvals for a busy executive. Some prefer fewer posts with more depth each.
  • A written result. Buyers who want a guarantee on reach, not only on output, compare agencies that put one in writing.

how to choose

  • Are you buying content, or a growth engine? If you also want paid ads, outbound and CRM work, an allbound team like Understory makes sense. If you want executive content and audience building, pay for that alone.
  • How many executives will post? Compare per-executive pricing with team retainers.
  • How much review can the executive do? Thirty posts a month means thirty approvals.
  • What will you measure? Ask each agency for a sample monthly report and for results from a company like yours.

when rallyup isn't the right fit

  • You want paid ads, outbound and RevOps from the same agency as your content. An allbound team fits better.
  • Your budget is under $5,000 a month per executive. A freelance ghostwriter is the better choice.
  • You want 20 or more posts a month per executive.
  • The executive can't give a 30-minute story interview twice a month.
