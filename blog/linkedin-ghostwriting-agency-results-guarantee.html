the four kinds of guarantee you'll see

Guarantee typeWhat it promisesHow easy to verifyWhat to watch
OutputA number of posts a month, a cadence, revisionsVery easyPosts can ship on time and still do nothing for you
SatisfactionA refund or rewrite if you don't like the workEasy, but subjectiveUsually limited to the first weeks or first drafts
Reach (impressions or followers)A number of impressions or followers in a set windowEasy: LinkedIn reports itWhether paid promotion counts, and who qualifies
Leads or meetingsA number of leads, calls or meetingsHarder: depends on definitionsWhat counts as a lead, and who books the meeting

None of these is wrong. They protect different things. An output guarantee protects your cadence. A reach guarantee protects the first result an executive program has to produce. A meetings guarantee usually belongs to outbound services, where the provider runs the outreach itself.

how to read a guarantee

  • The metric. Is it organic or does paid promotion count? Is it measured on the executive's personal profile or the company page?
  • The window. 90 days is enough time for a steady cadence to show. Much shorter windows push providers toward volume over quality.
  • Who qualifies. Guarantees are usually limited to a profile of client the provider knows it can help. That is a sign of honesty, not a catch.
  • The remedy. "You don't pay", a refund, or extra months of free work are very different outcomes. Get it in writing.
  • Your side of the deal. A guarantee assumes posts get approved and go live. If you go quiet for a month, no provider can hit a reach number.

questions to ask before you sign

  • What exactly is guaranteed, and how is it measured?
  • Does paid promotion count toward the number?
  • What do I have to do for the guarantee to hold?
  • What happens if it is missed: refund, no invoice, or more work?
  • Who writes my posts, and do I keep the same writer?
  • What does the monthly report show besides impressions?

frequently asked questions

do any linkedin ghostwriting agencies guarantee results?

Some do. Others commit to output (a number of posts, revisions, a cadence) rather than outcomes. rallyup guarantees 100k organic impressions in 90 days, or we keep working for free until you get there, for B2B founders and executives with $5m+ in ARR or funding. Whatever the offer, read the written terms: the metric, the time window, who qualifies and what happens if it is missed.

what should a linkedin ghostwriting guarantee cover?

The most useful guarantees name one measurable result, a fixed time window and a clear remedy. Impressions are the easiest result to verify because LinkedIn reports them on every post. Leads and meetings matter more to most buyers but depend on things the agency doesn't control, such as your offer and your sales follow-up.

why don't more agencies guarantee leads or meetings?

Because the agency controls the writing and the cadence, but not your offer, your pricing or how quickly your team follows up. A guarantee on reach is something an agency can stand behind. Leads come from what you do with that reach, which is why rallyup pairs content with ICP connection requests and reports impressions, follower growth and those connection requests every month.

is an impressions guarantee worth anything?

Yes, if the impressions are organic and reach the right people. Impressions are the first thing that has to move before profile views, connection requests and inbound messages can. In rallyup's 2026 benchmark, median monthly impressions grew 4.7x in the first 90 days (7 executives with complete data). Ask any agency whether paid promotion counts toward its number.
